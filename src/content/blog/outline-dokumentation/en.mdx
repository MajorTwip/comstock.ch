## Starting point

Documentation was, to be honest, never my favorite task and was neglected accordingly. About two years ago the need to actually track my progress came up.

After looking around I settled on [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md). With its huge range of plugins — and, more recently, the built-in "Bases" — it would still be my clear favorite. But I badly needed a web app so I could access my notes from systems where I can't install apps, let alone set up any kind of synchronization. And to collaborate with a friend and fellow self-hoster, [Outline](https://www.getoutline.com/) turned out to be the best fit.

## Setup

### Getting the basic app running

As a first step I just let it start once by creating a new project in my Docker manager, Arcane. I made a few changes to the provided docker-compose.yml:

- removed the entire nginx block and added the following labels instead, to get it routed through Traefik:

```yaml
labels:
  - traefik.enable=true
  - traefik.http.routers.doc.rule=Host("doc.comstock.ch")
  - traefik.http.routers.doc.tls=true
```

- switched the `redis:8` image to `redis:8-alpine` and `postgres:18` to `postgres:18-alpine`.

In the provided .env I fixed the URL and generated `SECRET_KEY` and `UTILS_SECRET` as described directly above those entries.

Outline doesn't ship its own authentication — a choice I really like, even though I understand it raises the bar for some people. Just to test things, I created a Google app, though I'm not sure that's a viable path for people without a Google Workspace.

### Adding Google SMTP to Outline

This turned out to be fairly easy once I discovered the app password has to go in **without spaces**. I just went to [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords), created a new app password, and updated the .env:

```ini
SMTP_SERVICE=Gmail
SMTP_USERNAME=myemail@example.com
SMTP_PASSWORD=theapppassfromggl
SMTP_FROM_EMAIL=My Doc Name <infra@example.com>
```

Even though `infra@example.com` is just an alias of my Google Workspace address, mail still gets delivered — but with the sender address `myemail@example.com`. The display name "My Doc Name", on the other hand, comes through correctly. I'm not yet sure why one works and the other doesn't.

### Adding Outline to Authentik

I just followed the guide at [integrations.goauthentik.io](https://integrations.goauthentik.io/documentation/outline/). The part about "Create an email verification scope mapping in authentik" was a bit tricky because of a broken link in the docs — searching the Authentik docs directly turns up the guide you actually need.

Even so, it just wouldn't work… and the logs weren't very illuminating at first glance. Then it clicked: I use my own Certificate Authority, installed on all my devices — but not inside the Docker containers.

> [!WARNING]
> When using an identity provider with a certificate not signed by a public CA, you need to add the custom CA into the container. New line in the .env: `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/var/lib/outline/data/ca.crt`

## Verdict

Outline was running smoothly after a short while, and with SMTP via Gmail and SSO through [Authentik](/en/blog/authentik-sso/) it feels like a proper, cleanly integrated piece of infrastructure.
